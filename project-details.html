LIVING ROOM PROJECT

Downtown Loft Makeover

A complete living room transformation involving the assembly of a large media console, a sectional sofa, and precision TV wall mounting on exposed brick.

Property Downtown Loft
Service Assembly & Mounting
Duration 4.5 Hours

The Process

01. Preparation

We began by protecting the hardwood floors and unboxing the large media console components. We mapped out the exact TV height and console placement to ensure perfect symmetry.

02. Assembly

The 4-piece media console was assembled and leveled. Given the uneven nature of the loft floor, precise adjustment of the unit's feet was critical.

03. Mounting

Mounting a 65" TV on exposed brick required specialized masonry anchors. We secured the bracket, mounted the display perfectly level, and concealed the cables using surface trunking painted to match the brick.
