SERVICE DETAILS

Wardrobe Assembly

Precision assembly for heavy, structural storage.

Large wardrobes require careful handling, perfect leveling, and secure wall anchoring to ensure they are safe and function smoothly for years.

We assemble all types of wardrobes, from standard two-door units to complex modular storage systems with sliding doors and internal lighting. Our installers ensure that doors are perfectly aligned, drawers glide smoothly, and the entire structure is safely secured to your wall to prevent tipping.

Service Facts

  • Est. Duration 2 - 4 Hours
  • Starting Price $150 / unit
  • Wall Anchoring Included
